Effective date: August 20, 2026 · ANDA is operated by Disode LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we", "us").
Updated on this date to describe how ANDA works today: the plan is monthly, payments are made by you from your own bank, and ANDA verifies that they arrived.

1. What ANDA is (and is not)

ANDA is a personal finance planning tool. It calculates a monthly plan to repay your credit card debt, tells you how much to pay and to which card, reminds you when a payment is due, and — for the cards you connect — verifies that your payment reached the card.

ANDA does not provide financial, legal, tax, or investment advice; all plans are mathematical projections based on the data available to us. ANDA does not move money: you make every payment yourself, from your own bank, to your own card issuer. ANDA never receives, holds, or transmits your funds.

ANDA is not a debt settlement, debt relief, debt adjustment, or credit repair service. We do not contact your creditors, do not negotiate on your behalf, do not act as an intermediary between you and your issuers, and do not receive payments from you to distribute to them. Your agreements with your card issuers are entirely between you and them.

If automatic transfers are offered in the future, they will be executed by a licensed payment processor under your explicit authorization, funds will move directly from your bank to your card issuer — never through ANDA's accounts — and these terms will be updated before that feature is enabled.

2. Eligibility and account

You must be at least 18 years old and reside in the United States. You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide, for keeping access to your email account secure, and for all activity under your ANDA account.

3. Bank connections

Connecting a financial institution is optional and read-only, powered by Plaid. You authorize us to retrieve, for the cards you connect: your credit card liabilities (balances, APRs, minimum payments, statement and due dates) and the transactions on those cards — which we use to verify that your payments arrived and to show your history. We never receive your bank username or password; those are handled directly by Plaid.

Data availability depends on your institution. Where your issuer does not report a figure, ANDA either leaves it blank and says so, or uses a clearly-labeled estimate that you can correct at any time. You can disconnect a bank whenever you want from Settings.

What we collect and how long we keep it is described in our Privacy Policy.

4. Payments are yours

When ANDA takes you to your bank's app or website to make a payment, it is only opening that app or page and, where possible, copying the amount to your clipboard so you don't have to retype it. You enter the payment, you confirm it, and you are responsible for it. ANDA cannot start, change, schedule, or cancel a payment.

When ANDA marks a payment as verified, it means we saw a matching transaction reported by your bank. That confirmation depends on your bank and can take several days to appear. A payment not yet shown as verified in ANDA may still have been made and received.

5. Projections and estimates

Payoff dates, interest totals, and interest avoided shown by ANDA are estimates that depend on the accuracy of the underlying data, your actual payments, and your issuer's terms. Actual results will vary. ANDA is not responsible for fees, interest, or penalties charged by your issuers, including late fees if a payment is made late or not made at all.

Reminders and notifications are a convenience, not a guarantee. They can be delayed or not delivered — by your device, your operating system, or your network — and it remains your responsibility to pay your issuers on time.

1. Qué es ANDA (y qué no es)

ANDA es una herramienta de planificación de finanzas personales. Calcula un plan mensual para pagar tus tarjetas de crédito, te dice cuánto pagar y a cuál tarjeta, te recuerda cuándo vence un pago y —en las tarjetas que conectes— verifica que tu pago llegó a la tarjeta.

ANDA no brinda asesoría financiera, legal, fiscal ni de inversión; los planes son proyecciones matemáticas basadas en los datos disponibles. ANDA no mueve dinero: cada pago lo haces tú, desde tu propio banco, a tu propio emisor. ANDA nunca recibe, retiene ni transmite tus fondos.

ANDA no es un servicio de liquidación de deudas, alivio de deudas, ajuste de deudas ni reparación de crédito. No contactamos a tus acreedores, no negociamos en tu nombre, no actuamos como intermediarios entre tú y tus emisores, y no recibimos pagos tuyos para distribuirlos entre ellos. Tus acuerdos con tus emisores son exclusivamente entre tú y ellos.

Si en el futuro se ofrecen transferencias automáticas, las ejecutará un procesador de pagos licenciado bajo tu autorización expresa, los fondos irán directo de tu banco a tu emisor — nunca por cuentas de ANDA — y estos términos se actualizarán antes de activar esa función.

Nos autorizas a obtener, de las tarjetas que conectes: tus deudas de tarjeta (balances, APRs, pagos mínimos, fechas de corte y de vencimiento) y los movimientos de esas tarjetas — que usamos para verificar que tus pagos llegaron y para mostrarte tu historial. Nunca recibimos tu usuario ni tu contraseña del banco; de eso se encarga Plaid directamente.

La disponibilidad de datos depende de tu institución. Cuando tu emisor no reporta un dato, ANDA lo deja en blanco y te lo dice, o usa un estimado claramente marcado que puedes corregir cuando quieras. Puedes desconectar un banco en cualquier momento desde Ajustes.

Cuando ANDA marca un pago como verificado, significa que vimos un movimiento coincidente reportado por tu banco. Esa confirmación depende de tu banco y puede tardar varios días en aparecer. Un pago que todavía no aparece verificado en ANDA puede haberse hecho y recibido igualmente.
