1. What ANDA is (and is not)

ANDA is a personal finance planning tool. It calculates a monthly plan to repay your credit card debt, tells you how much to pay and to which card, reminds you when a payment is due, and — for the cards you connect — verifies that your payment reached the card.

Your plan is an automated calculation based on your data and the strategy you choose in Plan (avalanche unless you change it). You can change the plan or ignore it at any time: whether, when, and how much you pay each creditor is always your decision.

ANDA does not provide financial, legal, tax, or investment advice of any kind; its plans are mathematical projections based on the data available to us. ANDA does not move money: you make every payment yourself, from your own bank, to your own card issuer. ANDA never receives, holds, or transmits your funds.

ANDA is not a debt settlement, debt relief, debt adjustment, debt management, credit counseling, or credit repair service, and it is not a credit service organization or a credit repair organization.
